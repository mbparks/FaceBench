## v3.1.0 precise arrangement

Live centering guides and snapping; per-axis faceplate centering; shape/group alignment to bounds or a fixed reference; even-center distribution; all four layer-order operations. Interaction regressions cover rotated/asymmetric geometry, grouped and locked selections, guide rendering commands, drag cancellation, Undo/Redo and persistence.

## v3.0.3 magnetic starters and deliberate opt-out

Every new face, starter and example has protected magnetic CAD backing. Fresh Start keeps compatible face preferences while restoring the Steam outline and backing. Custom unmounted plates require Advanced mode and confirmation; width/height edits cannot remove mounting. Actual starter UI, CAD/STL backing, upgrade recovery and opt-out behavior have regression coverage.

## v3.0.2 defaults and persistence

New projects use the Steam Machine magnetic CAD backing. Blank setup preferences are saved independently and reused by Fresh Start, with reload, upgrade, invalid-edit and storage-failure checks.

## v3.0.1 usability update

Outer face thickness is directly accessible in Inspector, with synchronized slider/numeric controls, undo/redo and specific feedback for conflicting engraving, bevel or pocket depths. Focused CAD and binary-STL checks confirm the front changes while the rear backing remains fixed.

## Product rules retained

Keep experiments reversible, random designs reproducible and projects portable. Keep source recipes available while editing. Provide meaningful numeric alternatives to canvas gestures. Place creation controls in Create/Inspector and manufacturing controls in Export. Keep geometry, units and operation labels consistent through the manufacturing handoff.

## Open validation work

1. Review desktop/mobile rendering, actual WebGL, keyboard/screen-reader behavior and modal focus in real browsers.
2. Confirm offline reload and IndexedDB recovery across browser restarts on the deployed host.
3. Import representative STL/3MF/SVG/DXF and material-stack outputs in actual slicer/cutter software; check raster SVG clipping and operation assignment.
4. Fabricate mounting and calibration coupons. Check the actual target machine, mounting hardware, clearance, airflow, retention and the effect of added material sheets.

The shipped software supports these checks; completion is not implied by a version number. The packages are not a deployment to mbparks.com.

## Deliberate limits

Brushes create discrete extrusions. Material appearances are illustrative, with no optical or thermal simulation. Text paths store a snapshot, and glyph outlines are warped along it. The custom material stack exports individual flat sheets, separate from the CAD-backed single plate. Remix is a local seeded generator and does not use an AI service. Collaboration, accounts, a public gallery and remote generative services are outside this release.
